# Code of Conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in ZIUAI repositories: employees, contractors, clients and outside contributors. It covers pull requests, issues, code review, discussions, chat channels and meetings connected to our projects.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and direct. Review the code, not the person. "This function is hard to follow because X" is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding. Most disagreements are missing context.
- Welcome questions at every level. Nobody is expected to know everything, and asking after 30 minutes stuck is the rule, not a weakness.
- Give credit. Say who did the work, including in commit messages and release notes.
- Keep private things private: customer data, client names under NDA, credentials and personal details stay out of public channels and issues.
- Disagree in the review, decide in the design doc, and then support the decision. Reopen it with new information, not by repeating the old argument.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, mockery or dismissive comments about someone's experience, background or identity.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately bypassing review, security rules or access controls, or pressuring others to do so.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting

Raise concerns with your tech lead or engineering manager, or email **admin@ziuai.ai**. Reports are handled confidentially, and the person reporting is told what happened next.

## Consequences

Maintainers and leads may remove comments, close pull requests or issues, and remove access for behaviour that breaks this code. Serious or repeated breaches are handled through the normal HR process.
